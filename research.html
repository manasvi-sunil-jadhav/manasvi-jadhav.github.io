IN PROGRESS

Do Corporate Disclosures Improve Stock Volatility Forecasts? Evidence from Explainable NLP and Machine Learning for Indian Listed Firms

My MSc Economics dissertation examines whether information contained in corporate disclosures can improve stock volatility forecasting for Indian listed firms.

The study combines financial text analysis and Natural Language Processing with machine learning and traditional volatility modelling. It focuses on extracting information from corporate disclosures and evaluating whether these textual signals provide additional information for forecasting future stock volatility.
