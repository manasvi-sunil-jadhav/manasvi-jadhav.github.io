Sales Performance Dashboard

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE · POWER BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse retail sales performance using the Sample Superstore dataset, with insights into sales, profitability, customers, products, and regional trends.

Project Overview

This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse retail sales performance using the Sample Superstore dataset.

The dashboard provides insights into sales, profit, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends to support data-driven business decisions.

Objectives

  • Analyse overall sales performance.
  • Monitor profitability across categories and regions.
  • Identify top-performing products and customers.
  • Explore regional and city-wise sales trends.
  • Build an interactive dashboard for business reporting.

Product Performance Dashboard

Dashboard Features

KPI Cards Total sales, total profit, total orders, total quantity, average order value, and profit margin.
Interactive Slicers Interactive filtering for exploring different aspects of the dataset.
Sales Trend Analysis Analysis of sales trends over time.
Regional Performance Analysis of sales and profitability across regions.
Product Performance Comparison of product-level performance.
Customer Analysis Analysis of customer contribution and performance.
City-wise Sales Analysis Exploration of sales patterns across cities.

Dataset

The dashboard uses the Sample Superstore dataset, which contains information on:

Orders Customers Products Sales Profit Discounts Shipping Geographic Location

Key Insights

  • Technology generated the highest sales.
  • Profitability varies across regions.
  • A small group of customers contributes significantly to revenue.
  • Product performance differs considerably across categories.
  • Interactive filtering enables detailed business analysis.
