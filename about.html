About Me

My Background

I completed my undergraduate degree in Economics in Mumbai and am currently pursuing an MSc in Economics with a specialisation in Data Analytics at Symbiosis School of Economics in Pune.

My academic background has given me an interest in economics, statistics, and econometrics, while my postgraduate studies have allowed me to explore how data and analytical methods can be used to study economic and business problems.

My Approach to Data

I enjoy working with data to identify patterns, relationships, and trends that can help explain a problem. I have worked with tools such as Python, SQL, R, Power BI, Excel, and SPSS through academic coursework and independent projects.

I am particularly interested in combining economic reasoning with quantitative and data-analytical methods rather than looking at data in isolation.

Beyond Academics

Outside academics, I have a strong interest in literature and reading, particularly in Marathi and English. My involvement in reading and literary activities has been an important part of my college experience.

I also enjoy activities that involve writing, editing, organising, and working with people, which have helped me develop skills beyond technical coursework.

I am continuing to build my skills in data analytics and research, with the aim of developing a career where economics and data can work together to answer meaningful questions.
